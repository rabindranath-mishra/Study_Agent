# NCERT Economics Class 11 — Chapter 4: Elasticity

> **Type:** Textbook | **Subject:** Economics | **Author:** NCERT
> **Reference:** NCERT, *Introductory Microeconomics*, Class 11, Chapter 4, pp. 48–63

---

## Summary

Chapter 4 introduces the concept of elasticity of demand and supply. It covers price elasticity of demand (PED), income elasticity of demand, and cross elasticity of demand. The chapter explains how to measure elasticity using the percentage method and provides real-world examples relevant to Indian markets.

---

## Key Concepts from This Source

- **Price Elasticity of Demand (PED):** Measures the responsiveness of quantity demanded to a change in price.
- **Formula:** PED = % Change in Quantity Demanded ÷ % Change in Price
- **Types of PED:** Perfectly elastic, elastic, unit elastic, inelastic, perfectly inelastic
- **Determinants of PED:** Nature of the good, availability of substitutes, proportion of income spent, time period
- **Income Elasticity of Demand:** Responsiveness of demand to a change in consumer income
- **Cross Elasticity of Demand:** Responsiveness of demand for one good to a change in the price of another

---

## Excerpts and Direct Quotes

> "The price elasticity of demand is defined as the ratio of the percentage change in demand to percentage change in price."
> — NCERT, *Introductory Microeconomics*, Class 11, p. 48

> "When the percentage change in demand is greater than the percentage change in price, demand is said to be elastic."
> — NCERT, *Introductory Microeconomics*, Class 11, p. 51
