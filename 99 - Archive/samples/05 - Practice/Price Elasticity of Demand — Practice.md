## Multiple Choice Questions

**1.** If the price of a good rises from ₹10 to ₹12 and quantity demanded falls from 100 to 80 units, what is the price elasticity of demand?

- A) 0.8
- B) 1.0
- C) 1.25
- D) 2.0

**2.** Which of the following goods is most likely to have perfectly inelastic demand?

**3.** When demand is elastic and price falls, total revenue:

- A) Falls
- B) Rises
- C) Remains unchanged
- D) Cannot be determined

**4.** On a linear (straight-line) demand curve, price elasticity of demand is:

- A) Constant throughout
- B) Greater at higher prices and lower at lower prices
- C) Greater at lower prices and lower at higher prices

## Fill in the Blanks

**10.** Complete the following:

a) When Ed = 0, demand is called ______________ inelastic.
b) When Ed > 1, a rise in price will cause total revenue to ______________.
c) The demand for a necessity like salt is ______________ inelastic because it has ______________ close substitutes.

---

## Answer Key

**MCQ Answers:**
1. **B — 1.0** *(% ΔQ = 20%, % ΔP = 20%, Ed = 1)*
2. **B — Insulin** *(no substitute, life-necessity)*
3. **B — Rises** *(elastic demand: price ↓ → Qd ↑ by larger %, TR rises)*
4. **B — Greater at higher prices** *(elasticity varies along a linear demand curve)*

**Fill in the Blanks:**
a) perfectly  b) fall  c) price, no  d) midpoint

**Q5 Model Answer:**
Price elasticity of demand is the ratio of the percentage change in quantity demanded to the percentage change in price of the good.
Formula: Ed = (% ΔQd) / (% ΔP) = (ΔQd/Qd) / (ΔP/P)

**Q9 Calculations:**
(a) Ed = (100/500) / (10/40) = 20% / 25% = **0.8** → Inelastic
(b) Ed = (100/400) / (10/50) = 25% / 20% = **1.25** → Elastic
