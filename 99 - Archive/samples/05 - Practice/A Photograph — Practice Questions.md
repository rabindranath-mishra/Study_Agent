# A Photograph — Practice Questions

> **Subject:** English | **Topic:** A Photograph — Shirley Toulson | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Time:** 25 minutes

---

## Short Answer (2-3 marks)

**Q1.** Who are Betty and Dolly in the poem?
> They are the poet's mother's cousins who appear in the old photograph, standing with her at the beach.

**Q2.** What does the photograph show?
> It shows the poet's mother as a twelve-year-old at a beach with cousins Betty and Dolly. The sea is in the background.

**Q3.** What did the mother find amusing when she looked at the photograph?
> She found her own childhood appearance amusing — her hairstyle and how the cousins looked.

**Q4.** What does "Both wry with the laboured ease of loss" mean?
> Both the mother's loss of her childhood and the poet's loss of her mother have become familiar — grief carried so long it is no longer raw, yet still present (paradox).

## Long Answer (6 marks)

**Q5.** Describe the three stages of time depicted in the poem.
> - **Past (~30 years ago):** Mother as a twelve-year-old at the beach with Betty and Dolly — carefree joy.
> - **Later past:** As an adult, mother laughs at her own old photo. Childhood is gone but memory brings amusement.
> - **Present:** Mother is dead. The poet can only feel silence. Even the memory of her mother's laughter is now a loss.

**Q6.** How does Shirley Toulson use the photograph as a symbol?
> The photograph symbolises frozen time — it preserves a moment forever while everything it represents has been lost. It becomes the poet's only remaining connection to her mother and a painful reminder that life flows while memories stay still.

## Figure of Speech

**Q7.** "The sea holiday was her past, mine is her laughter." — Identify the device.
> **Metaphor** — sea holiday and laughter are directly equated to "the past."

**Q8.** "Both wry with the laboured ease of loss." — Identify the device.
> **Paradox / Oxymoron** — "laboured ease" is contradictory; grief is both effortful and eventually comfortable.
