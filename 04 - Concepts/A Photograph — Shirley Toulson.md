# A Photograph — Shirley Toulson

## The Poem

> The cardboard shows me how it was
> When the two girl cousins went paddling,
> Each one holding one of my mother's hands,
> And she the big girl — some twelve years or so.
> All three stood still to smile through their hair
> At the uncle with the camera. A sweet face,
> My mother's, that was before I was born.
> And the sea, which appears to have changed less,
> Washed their terribly transient feet.
>
> Some twenty — thirty — years later
> She'd laugh at the snapshot. "See Betty
> And Dolly," she'd say, "and look how
> They dressed us for the beach." The sea holiday
> Was her past, mine is her laughter. Both wry
> With the laboured ease of loss.
>
> Now she has been dead nearly as many years
> As that girl in the photograph was old.
> And of this circumstance
> There is nothing to say at all.
> Its silence silences.

*Source: Hornbill, NCERT Class 11 English. Copyright Shirley Toulson / Pergamon Press.*

## Stanza-by-Stanza Summary

**Stanza 1** — The cardboard photograph shows the poet's mother as a young girl of twelve, standing at the beach with cousins Betty and Dolly holding her hands. The sea forms the backdrop.

**Stanza 2** — As an adult, the mother would laugh at her old photograph — her hairstyle, the cousins. That laughter is now silent; the mother has passed away.

**Stanza 3** — The poet has nothing to say. Both her mother and the memory of her laughter are gone. Silence is all that remains — a silence the poet has learned to live with.

## Key Themes
- **Transience of time** — time erases everything
- **Memory and loss** — photographs freeze moments but people pass away
- **Three-layered grief** — mother lost her childhood; poet lost her mother; grief matures into silence

## Important Lines
> "The sea holiday was her past, mine is her laughter."
The poet's loss mirrors the mother's loss.

> "Now she's been dead nearly as many years / As that girl in the photograph was old."
Time is measured in absence.

> "Its silence silences."
Grief becomes so heavy it silences even the act of expression.

## CBSE Exam Focus
- Three stanzas = three phases of grief (past joy, adult laughter, present silence)
- The photograph symbolises frozen time vs. flowing life
- The poem ends with acceptance, not bitterness
